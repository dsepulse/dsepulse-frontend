How the numbers are produced

Every trading day after the close, five tools run in sequence — each adding a layer of confirmation before a stock reaches the final list. Smart Money runs alongside them on volume and block trades.

  1. 014:35 PM

    Daily Picker

    Momentum scan — ranks every listed stock by price action, volume surge, category, PE and spread. Score out of 45.

  2. 025:00 PM

    WMA Analysis

    WMA 9/34/89 crossover signals from 150 days of history. Trend confirmation, with fresh-cross alerts.

  3. 035:15 PM

    Tech Analysis

    Full score out of 100 combining WMA, MACD 12/26/9, RSI 14, volume surge and Bollinger Bands.

  4. 045:30 PM

    Combined Signal

    Triple confirmation — only stocks where Picker ≥ 30, WMA = STRONG BUY and Tech ≥ 70 all agree.

  5. 055:30 PM

    BD Signal

    BD-optimised score out of 100 — DSEX mood, ATR stop loss, sector bonus, consecutive up days and a circuit check.
