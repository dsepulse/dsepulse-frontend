The problem

The market gives you too much information, and too much noise.

Hundreds of stocks trade on the Dhaka Stock Exchange, and every one of them produces a price, a volume, a chart and an opinion after the close. Almost none of that is a decision. Nothing in it tells you which name is worth an hour of your evening — or where you would get in, where you would take profit, and where you would be wrong.

How it works

Four steps, in the same order, every trading evening.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Hundreds of stocks are processed after the close — every listed name, not a watchlist someone chose in advance.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    Multiple market readings are evaluated on each one: momentum, moving averages, MACD, RSI, volume, Bollinger Bands, DSEX mood.

  3. 03

    Filter

    Only setups that meet the defined rules move forward. Everything that does not clear the thresholds is dropped, silently.

  4. 04

    Plan

    What survives is written as a setup you can act on or ignore: entry · target · risk.

You decide what to do with it.

What this is, and what it is not

The numbers are only worth something if you know exactly how they were arrived at. So here is the whole of it.

Rule-based

Arithmetic over past price and volume, and nothing else. The same rules run on every stock, every evening, with no discretion and no hand-picked exceptions. Nothing here predicts a price.

Conditional

Every taka figure on this site is an "if", not a forecast. It is what a position would come to if the price reached those levels — before brokerage, tax and slippage, and with no promise that any level is reached at all.
